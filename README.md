# ARENA
ARENA (Alignment Research Engineer Accelerator) is an intensive curriculum designed to upskill technical individuals to contribute directly to AI alignment and safety. They have in-person bootcamps as well as an open source learning curriculum available on their [official site](https://www.arena.education/).
Their online curriculum can be found [here](https://learn.arena.education/).
This repo is my learning journey through their curriculum. I have tried to execute all the exercises (on my own) to the best of my abilities and through it all I am learning a lot. 
Another effort I wish to make here is to compile all the amazing sources I have been finding while studying and keep a note of my understanding for some new concepts I come across. 

All in all, I am incredibly excited about the field of AI safety and hope to meaningfully contribute to it soon.
Happy learning! =D
